\section*{Summary}
This lab walked through the full CMOS layout flow: drawing and verifying an inverter, extending the same checks to NAND and a hierarchical ring oscillator, and closing the loop with DRC/LVS. We built simple testbenches, captured DC and transient waveforms, and compared pre- and post-layout behavior to see parasitic impact. The ring oscillator exercise reinforced device sizing, hierarchy, and clean routing, while the repeated DRC/LVS runs highlighted how small spacing or connectivity errors surface in the tools. Overall, I gained confidence in taking schematics to layout, validating them with Calibre-style checks, and interpreting waveforms to confirm functional intent.
